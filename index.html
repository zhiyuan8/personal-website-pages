I'm Zack Li. I build scalable AI systems end to end — from model training and software architecture to Gen AI inference that ships on device.

I'm a Senior Staff Machine Learning Engineer at Qualcomm, leading GenieX, the generative AI inference runtime that runs frontier models across Snapdragon NPU, GPU, and CPU. I also contribute to llama.cpp and support new GenAI models on Qualcomm® AI Hub.

I founded Nexa AI as CTO, building Nexa SDK (now GenieX) into a leading on-device AI runtime—#1 on GitHub Trending, #1 Product of the Day on Product Hunt, and 8k+ GitHub stars. I co-authored the Octopus and Omni model series—top-2 trending on Hugging Face and showcased at Google I/O and by Google DeepMind. I drove enterprise adoption with customers like HP and Lenovo, and led engineering delivery alongside partners including Qualcomm, AMD, NVIDIA, Microsoft, and more.

Before Nexa, I built on-device AI and software systems at Google, Amazon Lab126, and Cadence. I hold an M.S. from Stanford University (2019) and a B.E. from Tongji University (2017).
